Product types


In-ear earphones are the most popular product type on Amazon India (given that you need to make a purchase before you can give a rating), followed by USB cables, smartphones and HDMI cables.

Electronics, in general, seem to be the most popular category of products on Amazon India. It would be interesting to compare this other online sales platforms there.

Price and ratings

Amazon ratings, similar to other online reviews on other platforms, are heavily-skewed towards the high-end.




Most product sub-categories demonstrate very little relationship between price and rating.

For most product categories, higher prices do not always convert to higher product ratings





Discounts

Almost all products on Amazon India are discounted.



If anything, the most highly-discounted items are less satisfactory to customers (having slightly lower ratings). It could also be that unsatisfactory and unpopular products have also been discounted so they would sell.

Amongst non-discounted items (at 0% on the x-axis), there does seem to be a general range between 4 and 4.5 stars.
